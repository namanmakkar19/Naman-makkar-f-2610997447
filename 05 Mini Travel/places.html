Taj Mahal

A white marble mausoleum in Agra, built by Emperor Shah Jahan. It is one of the Seven Wonders of the World.

Jaipur City Palace

A palace complex in the Pink City of Rajasthan, known for its courtyards and museums.

Kerala Backwaters

A network of lagoons and canals in Kerala, popular for houseboat cruises.

Hampi

The ruins of the Vijayanagara Empire, set among boulder-strewn hills in Karnataka.

Ladakh

A high-altitude region with monasteries, deserts, and stunning mountain views.
