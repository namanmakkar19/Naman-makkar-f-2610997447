Introduction

Indian cuisine is known for its rich use of spices, regional variety, and vegetarian dishes. Every state has its own flavours, making food one of the best ways to experience the country.

Dish Descriptions

Butter Chicken
A creamy tomato-based curry with tender chicken pieces.
Masala Dosa
A crispy rice-and-lentil crepe filled with spiced potatoes.
Biryani
A fragrant rice dish cooked with spices, meat or vegetables.
